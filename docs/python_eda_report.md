## Findings

### F-001

- **Evidence:** Month-to-month customers: 3,875 customers, 1,655 churned, 42.7% churn rate.
- **Interpretation:** Flexible-contract customers are strongly associated with observed churn concentration.
- **Limitation:** Contract choice is not randomized and overlaps with tenure, pricing, and customer preferences.
- **Next action:** Compare contract groups within tenure bands and design a controlled offer test before claiming contract changes reduce churn.

### F-002

- **Evidence:** Customers in months 0-12: 2,186 customers, 1,037 churned, 47.4% churn rate.
- **Interpretation:** The early customer lifecycle is the clearest stage for deeper retention investigation.
- **Limitation:** Snapshot tenure is mechanically affected by survival; longer-tenure customers have already remained active longer.
- **Next action:** Measure onboarding milestones, early support contacts, and service issues, then test a defined early-tenure intervention.

### F-003

- **Evidence:** Fiber-optic customers: 3,096 customers, 1,297 churned, 41.9% churn rate.
- **Interpretation:** The fiber customer experience, pricing, or customer mix warrants investigation.
- **Limitation:** The dataset lacks service quality, outage, support-contact, promotion, and local-market variables.
- **Next action:** Join service-quality and support data before selecting an operational intervention.

### F-004

- **Evidence:** Electronic-check customers: 2,365 customers, 1,071 churned, 45.3% churn rate.
- **Interpretation:** Payment friction or characteristics correlated with electronic checks may be relevant.
- **Limitation:** Payment method may proxy for contract, tenure, digital behavior, or customer preference.
- **Next action:** Compare payment methods within contract and tenure groups; test a payment-experience change with a control group.

### F-005

- **Evidence:** Median monthly charges are 79.65 for churned versus 64.43 for retained customers; median total charges are 703.55 versus 1683.60.
- **Interpretation:** Churned customers tend to have higher current charges but lower accumulated charges, consistent with shorter tenure.
- **Limitation:** The distributions overlap, and total charges are strongly exposure-dependent because they accumulate with tenure.
- **Next action:** Compare monthly charges within service and tenure groups; do not treat an unadjusted average difference as a price effect.
